# Arihanth Jewelry Management System - User Guide

Welcome to the Arihanth Jewelry Management System! This guide maps out the end-to-end processes for each role, broken down screen by screen so you know exactly where to go and what to do.

---

## 1. Super Admin Workflow

The Super Admin has full oversight over the entire platform.

**1. Login Screen**
*   **Process: Login (End-to-End)**
    1. Open the app and navigate to the Login screen.
    2. Enter your registered Super Admin email or mobile number.
    3. Enter your secure password.
    4. Tap **Login** to access the system dashboard.
*   **Process: Password Recovery (End-to-End)**
    1. Tap the **Forgot Password?** link on the login screen.
    2. Enter your registered email or mobile number and tap submit.
    3. You will receive a One-Time Password (OTP).
    4. Enter the OTP into the app to verify your identity.
    5. Set and confirm your new password, then log in.

**2. Finance & Analytics Dashboard Screen**
*   **Process: Monitor Global Metrics (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Dashboard** from the main menu.
    2. View the high-level summary cards (e.g., Total Revenue, Pending Orders).
    3. Tap on specific charts or metrics to drill down into detailed production statistics.
    4. Use the date filters at the top to adjust the reporting period.

**3. Settings & Configuration Screen**
*   **Process: Master Data Management (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Settings** or **Configuration** module.
    2. Select the specific setting category (e.g., Metal Rates, App Rules).
    3. If necessary, disable automatic live metal rates and manually input override values.
    4. Tap **Save Changes** to immediately apply the settings globally.

**4. Global Orders (PO/SO/WO) & Repairs Screens**
*   **Process: System Oversight (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the respective module (Purchase Orders, Work Orders, etc.).
    2. Use the search or filter tools to find specific orders across all users.
    3. Tap on any order to view its full lifecycle and current status.
    4. If needed, manually intervene by overriding the status or reassigning the order to a different user.

## 2. Key User Workflow

Key Users oversee business partners, approve orders, and manage daily operations.

**1. Login Screen**
*   **Process: Login (End-to-End)**
    1. Open the app and enter your Key User credentials.
    2. Tap **Login** to access the Key User dashboard.
*   **Process: Password Recovery (End-to-End)**
    1. Tap **Forgot Password?**.
    2. Receive and verify the OTP sent to your contact info.
    3. Reset your password and log in.

**2. Business Partners Screen**
*   **Process: Partner Creation (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Business Partners** screen.
    2. Tap the **+ Add New Partner** button.
    3. Tap the **Scan Document (OCR)** icon to open the camera.
    4. Capture the partner's KYC document (e.g., ID card or business license).
    5. Allow the app to automatically extract and fill in the text fields.
    6. Verify the auto-filled information, make corrections if necessary, and tap **Save**.
*   **Process: Partner Edit (End-to-End)**
    1. Open the **Business Partners** list.
    2. Use the search bar to locate the partner.
    3. Tap the partner's card, then tap the **Edit** icon.
    4. Update the necessary fields (e.g., new address or phone number).
    5. Tap **Update** to save the changes to the database.

**3. Meetings Screen**
*   **Process: Client Engagement (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Meetings** screen.
    2. Tap **+ Schedule Meeting**.
    3. Select the Business Partner, set the Date and Time, and input the Agenda.
    4. Tap **Save** to create the calendar event.
    5. After the meeting concludes, open the event again to type in the Meeting Minutes and outcomes, then tap **Update**.

**4. Designs & Products Screens**
*   **Process: Manage Designs (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Designs** screen.
    2. Tap **+ Add Design**.
    3. Upload high-quality reference images from your gallery or camera.
    4. Input specifications (e.g., expected weight, metal type, stones required).
    5. Tap **Save**. This blueprint is now available for craftsmen.
*   **Process: Manage Stock & Prices (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Products** screen.
    2. Tap on an existing product or add a new one.
    3. Assign the product to a category (e.g., Rings, Necklaces).
    4. View the suggested price based on **Live Metal Rates**, adjust the markup if needed, and tap **Save** to make it visible in the Digital Catalogue.

**5. Purchase Orders (PO) & Work Orders (WO) Screens**
*   **Process: Approve Orders (SO Creation) (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Purchase Orders** screen.
    2. Review the list of "Pending" POs submitted by Buyers.
    3. Tap on a PO to verify the requested items and quantities.
    4. Tap **Approve**. This automatically generates a confirmed **Sales Order (SO)** and notifies the Buyer.
*   **Process: Assign Production (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Work Orders** screen.
    2. Tap **+ Add Work Order** to convert an approved order into a production task.
    3. Select the relevant **Sales Order** or manually input the design/product details.
    4. Select a specific **Craftsman** from the dropdown list to assign the task.
    5. Set the expected deadline and add any specific instructions.
    6. Tap **Save**. The WO is dispatched to the Craftsman's queue.

## 3. Buyer Workflow

Buyers are clients or retail partners who browse catalogues and place orders.

**1. Login Screen**
*   **Process: Login (End-to-End)**
    1. Open the app and enter your Buyer credentials.
    2. Tap **Login**.
*   **Process: Password Recovery (End-to-End)**
    1. Tap **Forgot Password?**.
    2. Receive and verify your OTP, reset the password, and log in.

**2. Digital Catalogue / Products Screen**
*   **Process: Place a Purchase Order (PO) (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Digital Catalogue** or **Products** screen.
    2. Scroll through or use the search bar to find desired jewelry items.
    3. Tap on an item to view its details and pricing.
    4. Specify the required quantity and tap **Add to Cart**.
    5. Navigate to your Cart and review the summary.
    6. Tap **Checkout** or **Submit Order**. A **Purchase Order (PO)** is generated and sent to the Key User for approval.

**3. Dashboard / My Orders Screen**
*   **Process: Track Progress (End-to-End)**
    1. Open your **Dashboard** or **My Orders** screen.
    2. Locate your recent PO in the list.
    3. View the color-coded status indicator (e.g., Pending, Approved, In Production, Ready for Delivery).
    4. Tap the order for a detailed timeline of its progression.

**4. Work Orders (WO) Screen**
*   **Process: Create Work Order (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Work Orders** screen from the main menu.
    2. Tap the **+ Add Work Order** (or New) button.
    3. Select the specific **Design** or **Product** you want to request.
    4. Fill in the required specifications such as metal type, desired weight, and target quantity.
    5. Optionally, add any custom notes or attach reference images.
    6. Tap **Submit** or **Save**. The Work Order is now created and its status will appear as "Pending".

**5. Repairs Screen**
*   **Process: Request Repair (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Repairs** screen.
    2. Tap **+ New Repair Request**.
    3. Select the item or upload a photo of the damaged jewelry.
    4. Type a description of the issue (e.g., "broken clasp", "needs polishing").
    5. Tap **Submit**. You can check this screen later to track the repair status.

## 4. Craftsman Workflow

Craftsmen are head artisans managing production tasks and delegating to staff.

**1. Login Screen**
*   **Process: Login (End-to-End)**
    1. Open the app and enter your Craftsman credentials.
    2. Tap **Login**.
*   **Process: Password Recovery (End-to-End)**
    1. Tap **Forgot Password?**, verify your OTP, reset, and log in.

**2. Work Orders (WO) Screen**
*   **Process: Production Workflow (End-to-End)**
    1. On the **Work Orders** screen, tap on a newly assigned WO to view its details and blueprints.
    2. Tap the **Status** dropdown and change it from "Pending" to **"In Progress"** as you begin manufacturing.
    3. If you have staff, tap **Delegate** to assign specific sub-tasks to your Craftsman Staff.
    4. Update the WO periodically with materials used (e.g., gold weight, stones).
    5. Once the physical item is finished, change the Status to **"Completed"** and tap **Save**. This notifies the Key User that the item is ready.

**3. Repairs Screen**
*   **Process: Process Repairs (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to the **Repairs** screen.
    2. View the queue of items sent in by Buyers.
    3. Tap on a repair ticket, review the requested fixes, and mark it as **"In Progress"**.
    4. Once the jewelry is fixed, log any materials used and update the status to **"Resolved"** or **"Completed"**.

## 5. Craftsman Staff Workflow

Craftsman Staff assist with specific production tasks on the shop floor.

**1. Login Screen**
*   **Process: Login (End-to-End)**
    1. Open the app and enter your Staff credentials.
    2. Tap **Login**.
*   **Process: Password Recovery (End-to-End)**
    1. Tap **Forgot Password?**, verify your OTP, reset, and log in.

**2. Assigned Tasks (WO) Screen**
*   **Process: Execute Sub-Tasks (End-to-End)**
    1. Navigate to your **Assigned Tasks** or **Work Orders** screen.
    2. Review the specific portions of the WO delegated to you by the head Craftsman.
    3. Tap on a task and update its status to **"In Progress"**.
    4. Log the exact time spent or specific materials you used for your portion.
    5. Tap **Mark as Completed** when your specific sub-task is done, alerting the head Craftsman to review your work.
